# Original P1 placeholder recordings

The six PCM WAV recordings in this directory were synthesized for World of Words
from simple sine waves by tools/generate_placeholder_audio.py. No sampled or
third-party recording was used. They are dedicated to the public domain under
CC0-1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ .

These are provisional effects, not the final sound direction. Audibility and
comfort on the reference Android remain part of Chris's device QA.
